Auto-replies

The AI looks at photos customers send: a product they want, a screenshot of your post, a payment receipt, a damaged item. Works with both Claude and ChatGPT, no extra key. The 3 most recent photos in the chat are included, which adds a little to the cost of each reply.
The first time the AI talks to a customer, it reads their whole chat once and saves a profile (name, address, likes, complaints). After that it only reads the recent messages plus the profile, which is faster and cheaper.
